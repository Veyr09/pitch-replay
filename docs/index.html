Pitch replay — a full match from a sparse event log

The log holds one pitch position per event, a few seconds apart. Everything between two events is invented here: the ball travels at a plausible speed and then dwells, and the other twenty-one players are a pure function of the clock, the formation and the ball. Nothing is simulated frame to frame, so seeking is exact — drag the scrubber and the picture matches what playing to that minute would have shown. Same seed, same replay.

TypeScript and PixiJS 8, no framework and no backend; the whole thing mounts into one container element. Source: src/log.ts (the log shape), src/state.ts (the interpolation, which is the interesting part) and src/viewer.ts (pitch, camera, HUD, minimap).
